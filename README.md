## 🎭 What is Black Box?

> Black Box is the vault for the curious — a place where devs and hackers drop their tools, trade knowledge, and push the limits of what's possible.
>
> No noise, just pure innovation.

This isn't your typical repo. It's a space built for people who open things up just to see how they tick — builders, breakers, tinkerers, and everyone in between.

<br/>

## ⚡ What you'll find here

- 🛠️ **Tools** — built, broken, and rebuilt better
- 🧠 **Knowledge** — shared without gatekeeping
- 🔓 **Experiments** — the kind that push limits
- 🤝 **A community** — curious minds, zero noise
